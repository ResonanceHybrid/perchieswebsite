My pets don't appear over other apps

  • Open Perchies, go to Modes and turn on float mode.
  • When your phone asks, allow Perchies to show over other apps.
  • On some phones, also let Perchies run in the background in your battery settings.

How do I send them home?

Tap Call home in the app, or open Perchies and switch float mode off.
