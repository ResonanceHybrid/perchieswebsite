Questions

Still curious? Get in touch.

Is Perchies free?

Yes, Perchies is free to download on Google Play.

Will the pets get in my way?

They stay along the edges of your screen, and taps that miss a pet go straight through to your app. Call them home with one tap whenever you want them gone.

What permissions does it need?

Display over other apps, so the pets can walk on your screen in float mode. Android also shows a small notification while your pets are out roaming.

Does it collect my data?

No. There is no account and no tracking. Your squad, treats and reminders stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.

Is it on iPhone or my computer?

Perchies is on Android today. A web and desktop version is on the way, with room for an even bigger squad.
