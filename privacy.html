Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Short version: Perchies collects nothing about you. No account, no ads, no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Perchies remembers a few things so your pets are just how you left them:

  • Which pets are in your squad
  • When you last fed them
  • Your reminders
  • Your settings

This stays on your phone. We never see it. Uninstall the app and it's gone.

What we don't collect

Nothing personal. Not your name, email, contacts, location, photos or files. There are no ads and nothing that follows you around.

Why Perchies asks for permission

  • Show over other apps so your pets can walk on your screen. Perchies can't see what's in your other apps.
  • Notifications so your phone can show that your pets are out.

Updates and ratings

Updates and the "rate this app" pop-up come from Google Play. Google's own privacy policy covers those.

This website

No cookies, no ads and no tracking. The pets here remember your squad in your own browser, and nothing is sent to us.

Kids

Perchies is for everyone. Since we collect nothing, we collect nothing from kids either.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page first.
